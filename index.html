Privacy Policy for Goetic Companion

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Goetic Companion does not collect any personal data. There's no account and no sign-in, there's no server belonging to the developer that the app talks to, and there's no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Nothing you write or do in the app is sent anywhere, and nothing about you is ever shared or sold.

The app does include one in-app purchase, which is handled by Google Play. That's described in full under In-app purchase below.

What the app stores

Your Journal entries, favorites, and settings (like night mode or text size) are stored only on your device, in a private local database. So is the record that you've bought the unlock, if you have. The developer never receives any of this information. There's no account and no sign-in.

Backup

Like most Android apps, Goetic Companion is included in Android's standard Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, your Journal is included in your own Google account backup, the same as your other apps' data. On Android 9 and later, if your phone has a screen lock set (a PIN, pattern, or password), this backup is end-to-end encrypted using that screen lock, so Google itself can't read it — and neither can the developer. The same applies if Android transfers your data directly to a new device. The developer has no access to any of this.

Journal Lock

If you turn on the optional Journal Lock setting, unlocking your Journal uses your device's own fingerprint, face, or screen-lock prompt. This is handled entirely by Android — the app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data, or device credential. It only gets a yes or no answer.

PDF exports

If you choose to export a printable working guide or equipment kit, that PDF is created on your device and shared only through Android's own share sheet, to wherever you choose to send it. Nothing is exported or shared unless you ask for it.

In-app purchase

Goetic Companion offers a single one-time purchase that unlocks the full app. The purchase is made through Google Play, using Google Play's own purchase screen. The developer never sees your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no name. Google handles the payment, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to it.

Google Play records the purchase against your own Google account, the same as any other Play purchase. This is your purchase history, and it is why the app's Data safety section on Google Play declares Purchase history as collected. It is not shared with anyone, it is required rather than optional, and it is used only for app functionality — specifically, so the app can tell that you've bought the unlock, including after you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. It is never used for advertising, never analysed, and never sold.

What the app itself receives back from Google Play is simply whether that purchase exists. It stores that one yes-or-no answer on your device so the app stays unlocked, and that cached answer never leaves your phone. The app does not create an account for you, does not keep a copy of your purchase history, and does not send the purchase, or anything else, to the developer.

Network access

Google's Play Billing Library, which the app uses for that purchase, brings its own network components with it, so the installed app carries Android's internet permission. Those components belong to Google and are used by Google for the billing system and its own diagnostics; Google's Privacy Policy covers them. The app's own code does not use the network — there is no server on the developer's side for it to contact, and no part of your Journal, favorites, or settings is ever transmitted.

Age

Goetic Companion is intended for adults 18 and older.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored at any time, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling it. If you've bought the unlock, clearing storage also clears the app's local record of it — but the purchase itself stays with your Google account, and the app restores the unlock from Google Play the next time it runs. Your purchase history on Google Play is held by Google, not by the developer, so it's managed through your Google account rather than through this app.

Changes to this policy

If what the app does with data ever changes, this policy will be updated to match before that update ships.
