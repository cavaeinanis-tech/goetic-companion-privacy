Privacy Policy for Goetic Companion

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Goetic Companion does not collect any personal data. The app has no internet access at all — it cannot send anything from your device anywhere, because it isn't built with the permission to do so. There's no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What the app stores

Your Journal entries, favorites, and settings (like night mode or text size) are stored only on your device, in a private local database. The developer never receives this information. There's no account, no sign-in, and no server this app talks to.

Backup

Like most Android apps, Goetic Companion is included in Android's standard Auto Backup. If backup is turned on for your phone, your Journal is included in your own Google account backup, the same as your other apps' data. On Android 9 and later, this backup is end-to-end encrypted using your own device credential, so Google itself can't read it — and neither can the developer. The same applies if Android transfers your data directly to a new device. The developer has no access to any of this.

Journal Lock

If you turn on the optional Journal Lock setting, unlocking your Journal uses your device's own fingerprint, face, or screen-lock prompt. This is handled entirely by Android — the app never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data, or device credential. It only gets a yes or no answer.

PDF exports

If you choose to export a printable working guide or equipment kit, that PDF is created on your device and shared only through Android's own share sheet, to wherever you choose to send it. Nothing is exported or shared unless you ask for it.

Age

Goetic Companion is intended for adults 18 and older.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has stored at any time, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds in-app purchases — this policy will be updated to match before that update ships.
